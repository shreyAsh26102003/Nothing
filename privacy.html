Privacy and Nothing.

Nothing. is a small browser game by After All. Playing does not require a real name or email address.

What we collect

We store a random browser/player identifier, your rounds and scores, leaderboard display name if you choose one, and basic events such as game starts, replays and shares. For challenge links, we may record a referral identifier. If you separately opt in for product updates, we store your email address, consent, and delivery/confirmation status. An address is treated as verified only after the confirmation link is used. If email delivery is not configured or the link is not confirmed, your address remains unverified.

What we use it for

To operate the game, calculate rankings, prevent automated or fraudulent score submissions, understand performance, and send After All updates only if you opt in. We do not use gameplay to determine your real identity.

Who processes it

Our hosting and database providers include Vercel and Supabase. When email delivery is enabled, Resend processes email address and message delivery details to send requested confirmation emails. The game keeps a player credential in your browser's local storage so your ranking can persist across visits on that browser. Clearing browser data can remove access to the same leaderboard identity.

Leaderboard and rewards

Display names and scores are public. Ranking in the game does not automatically prove identity or establish eligibility for any future prizes. Any future rewards will require separately published rules and winner verification.

Your choices

Joining the updates list is optional. You can request access, corrections or deletion by contacting After All through @becauseafterall. We may need to confirm you control the relevant player identity before acting on a score request.

Manage your player data

If you're using the same browser where you played, you can export your latest 100 round records and current score, or permanently delete your player identity and associated email opt-in. Anonymous usage aggregates can remain.

Retention

We retain gameplay records while the experience is operating or they are needed for security and audit purposes, subject to applicable law and deletion requests. Email subscribers can unsubscribe using the link in a confirmation email or manage their preferences through the signup page. They can also ask to delete their information.

Last updated 27 November 2026. This statement describes the preview's data practices; it should undergo legal review before a broader public campaign.
